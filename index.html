Power of Protein

Protein is the building block of every cell. Here's why it deserves a spot on every plate.

💪

Builds Lean Muscle

Protein supplies the amino acids your muscles need to repair, recover, and grow stronger after every workout.

🔥

Boosts Metabolism

Digesting protein burns more calories than carbs or fat, naturally raising your daily energy expenditure.

🍽️

Curbs Cravings

High-protein meals keep you full for longer, reducing late-night snacking and unwanted sugar dips.

🦴

Strengthens Bones

Adequate protein intake supports bone density and lowers the risk of fractures as you age.

🧠

Sharpens the Mind

Amino acids are precursors to neurotransmitters that regulate focus, mood, and mental clarity.

🩹

Speeds Recovery

From everyday wear-and-tear to post-workout soreness, protein helps tissues rebuild faster.

Top Protein Sources

Hitting your target is easier with high-quality, protein-dense foods.

🍗

Chicken Breast

~31 g per 100 g — lean, versatile, and a classic muscle-building staple.

🥚

Eggs

~6 g per egg — complete amino acid profile and rich in choline.

🐟

Salmon / Tuna

~25 g per 100 g — adds omega-3s for heart and brain health.

🥛

Greek Yogurt

~10 g per 100 g — gut-friendly, with calcium and probiotics.

🫘

Lentils & Beans

~9 g per 100 g cooked — fiber-rich plant protein for any diet.

🥜

Paneer / Cottage Cheese

~18 g per 100 g — slow-digesting casein, perfect before bed.

Why Exercise Wins

Every rep, step, and stretch compounds into a healthier, sharper, longer life.

❤️

Healthier Heart

Cardio strengthens the heart muscle, lowers blood pressure, and improves cholesterol balance.

😊

Mood Lift

Exercise triggers endorphins and serotonin — natural antidotes to stress, anxiety, and low mood.

⚡

More Energy

Regular movement improves mitochondrial efficiency so daily tasks feel lighter and less draining.

🛌

Deeper Sleep

Active people fall asleep faster and spend more time in restorative deep sleep stages.

🛡️

Stronger Immunity

Moderate exercise improves immune cell circulation, helping you fend off infections.

🎯

Better Focus

Even a 20-minute walk boosts memory, creativity, and concentration for hours afterward.
